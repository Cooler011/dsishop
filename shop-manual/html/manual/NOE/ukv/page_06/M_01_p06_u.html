6. Account Activity
Your Account Activity lists all your activity in the Nintendo DSi Shop, such as downloads and Nintendo DSi Points purchases. To view these details, touch SETTINGS on the Nintendo DSi Shop Main Menu, then select ACCOUNT ACTIVITY.

Touch a software title to display more information about that title. If you have deleted software, you can also download it again for free. Touch CREDIT CARD to display the Receipt Screen.

For information on removing Account Activity, see "8. Settings/Other".
