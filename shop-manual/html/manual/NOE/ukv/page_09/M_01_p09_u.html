9. Parental Controls
Using Parental Controls, parents and guardians can determine which software can be used and downloaded, and also which software can be played on the Nintendo DSi system.

With Parental Controls set, parents and guardians can control which software titles can be downloaded from the Nintendo DSi Shop, based on the title’s rating. They can also block the usage of Nintendo DSi Points by their children and restrict usage of online communication functions.

For more information, please refer to the Parental Controls section of the Nintendo DSi Operations Manual - Applications and Settings.

Note:
Even if the use of Nintendo DSi Points is restricted by your Parental Controls settings, it will still be possible to download titles which do not require Nintendo DSi Points to download.
