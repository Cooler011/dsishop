5. Adding Nintendo DSi Points
Nintendo DSi Points are required to download certain content from the Nintendo DSi Shop. Nintendo DSi Points can be added to your account either by purchasing them using a credit card, or by redeeming a Nintendo eShop Card. Touch ADD NINTENDO DSi POINTS on the Main Menu to display the Add Nintendo DSi Points Screen, then touch the appropriate button.
Note:
Nintendo DSi Points can also be added using a Nintendo Points Card or Wii Points Card.
