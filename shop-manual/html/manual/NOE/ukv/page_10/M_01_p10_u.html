10. Q & A
Q:
What is available in the Nintendo DSi Shop?
A:
Software made exclusively for use on Nintendo DSi systems can be downloaded.

Q:
What are the payment options in the Nintendo DSi Shop?
A:
Software in the Nintendo DSi Shop is purchased with Nintendo DSi Points. You can purchase Nintendo DSi Points using a Visa or MasterCard credit card. You can also redeem a Nintendo eShop Card for Nintendo DSi Points. In addition to these methods, Wii Points Cards and Nintendo Points Cards can still be redeemed for Nintendo DSi Points.

Q:
Where can I purchase Nintendo eShop Cards?
A:
You can obtain a Nintendo eShop Card at many retailers that carry Nintendo products.

Note: Nintendo Points and Nintendo DSi Points have no monetary value and cannot be redeemed for cash. All Nintendo DSi Points and software downloads are only for use on a single Nintendo DSi system and are non-refundable and non-transferable to other Nintendo DSi systems.

Q:
Can I still use a Wii Points Card or a Nintendo Points Card?
A:
Yes, a Wii Points Card or a Nintendo Points Card can still be used in the same way as a Nintendo eShop Card to redeem Nintendo DSi Points for use in the Nintendo DSi Shop.

Q:
How do I use a Nintendo eShop Card?
A:
Scratch off the silver area on the back of the card to reveal the Nintendo eShop Card activation number. This number can then be entered in the Add Nintendo DSi Points section of the Nintendo DSi Shop.

Q:
Which credit cards can I use with Nintendo DSi Shop?
A:
Either Visa or MasterCard credit cards can be used.

Q:
Are instruction manuals available for software titles downloaded from the Nintendo DSi Shop?
A:
Yes, an electronic instruction manual is available for each title downloaded from the Nintendo DSi Shop. After the software title has been purchased, the instruction manual can be viewed from within the software.

Q:
Can I return something I've downloaded from the Nintendo DSi Shop?
A:
All software titles downloaded through the Nintendo DSi Shop are non-refundable and non-transferable.

Q:
How do I make more blocks available in the Nintendo DSi System Memory?
A:
Content downloaded from the Nintendo DSi Shop is saved to the Nintendo DSi System Memory. To see how many free blocks are available, touch the SYSTEM SETTINGS icon in the Nintendo DSi Menu and choose DATA MANAGEMENT. If the content you want to download requires more blocks than you have available, you will need to manage your content.

Note: To free up blocks, you can move software data to an SD Card. You can also delete software to which you no longer need immediate access. If you decide to delete software, you can always return to the Nintendo DSi Shop and re-download it at no additional cost, provided that you have not removed your Nintendo DSi Shop Account since downloading the software.

IMPORTANT: Deleting software data results in deleting save data as well, which cannot be recovered by re-downloading the software.

Q:
Is the selection of software available from the Nintendo DSi Shop going to increase over time?
A:
Yes, the selection is going to be continually expanded.

Q:
Is there a fee for
re-downloading deleted
software titles?
A:
No, deleted software titles can be downloaded again for free.

Q:
Where are the software titles purchased from the Nintendo DSi Shop stored?
A:
They are stored in the System Memory of the Nintendo DSi.

Q:
Where is the save data for software titles purchased from the Nintendo DSi Shop stored?
A:
The save data is stored in the Nintendo DSi System Memory as part of the software purchased. Please note that, if the software is deleted, the save data will be deleted as well.

Q:
Can I back up software and save data to an SD Card?
A:
Yes, but the data stored on the SD Card cannot be used with another Nintendo DSi system.

Q:
What do the UPDATED! icon on the software banner and the UPDATE button on the in the Nintendo DSi Shop mean?
A:
The UPDATED! icon indicates that there are enhancements available for the software title. If you have already downloaded the title, you may then select UPDATE from the Details Screen to download the enhanced version.

Q:
I formatted my Nintendo DSi System Memory via the System Settings. Can I get my content back?
A:
Yes. To get back any Nintendo DSi Shop content you have lost by formatting your Nintendo DSi System Memory, touch SETTINGS from the Nintendo DSi Shop Main Menu and select TITLES YOU’VE DOWNLOADED to re-download any software at no additional cost.

Q:
Can I transfer or share Nintendo DSi Shop content with other Nintendo DSi systems?
A:
All Nintendo DSi Points and software downloads are only for use on a single Nintendo DSi system and are non-refundable and non-transferable.

Q:
I downloaded a software title from the Nintendo DSi Shop, but it isn’t working properly. How do I get it fixed?
A:
If you experience problems with software downloaded from the Nintendo DSi Shop, you can delete and re-download the software free of charge. If any enhancements or fixes for a specific title become available in the Nintendo DSi Shop, you will see an icon (UPDATED!) on the software banner and an UPDATE option on the title’s Details Screen. If you continue to experience problems, please contact your local Nintendo Service Centre for assistance.

Q:
How can I remove my Nintendo DSi Shop Account?
A:
Touch SETTINGS from the Nintendo DSi Shop Main Menu. Then, go to the Settings/Other Screen and select REMOVE NINTENDO DSi SHOP ACCOUNT.

Note: If you remove your Nintendo DSi Shop Account, all of your registered Nintendo DSi Points and downloaded software will be deleted. You will no longer be able to re-download the software free of charge.

Note: If your Nintendo DSi system is transferred to another person, your registered Nintendo DSi Points and the software you have downloaded cannot be transferred, so your Nintendo DSi Shop Account will first have to be removed.

IMPORTANT: Once your Nintendo DSi Shop Account has been removed, you will not be able to recover it.

Q:
How do the Parental Controls work?
A:
The Parental Controls settings of the Nintendo DSi allow you to manage the content that is accessible to users of your Nintendo DSi system. If you apply Parental Controls, you will be prompted to set a 4-digit PIN which will allow you to adjust the Parental Controls settings in future, and also allow you to access content that may be restricted by the Parental Controls settings. The first time you select the Parental Controls option, the system will ask you to adjust the settings in order to specify which will be the HIGHEST GAME RATING ALLOWED.

Q:
Will my purchases through the Nintendo DSi Shop earn me Club Nintendo Stars?
A:
Stars will not be added automatically when a software title is purchased from the Nintendo DSi Shop, but will instead be added once the optional survey on the Club Nintendo web page has been answered. In order to take advantage of this feature, your Club Nintendo membership must be linked with your Nintendo DSi Shop Account. For details, please see www.club-nintendo.com.
