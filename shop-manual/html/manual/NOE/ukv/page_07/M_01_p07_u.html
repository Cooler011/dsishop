7. Titles You’ve Downloaded
Touch SETTINGS on the Nintendo DSi Shop Main Menu, then select TITLES YOU'VE DOWNLOADED to display the Titles You've Downloaded Screen.

You can re-download deleted software and download updates for existing software currently on the system here. Touch the software banner to go to the Details Screen and select either DOWNLOAD or UPDATE.

Note:
Titles may be withdrawn without prior notification due to unforeseen circumstances. In the event of this happening, you will no longer be able to re-download titles that have been deleted from your console.
